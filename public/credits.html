AARADHYA is a demonstration storefront for a Diwali saree collection. Product names, prices and stock are illustrative and come from a JSON catalogue. The people pictured do not endorse AARADHYA, and the photographs do not show products sold by any real business.

Maa Lakshmi

The goddess in the opening, the hero, the Jai Maa Lakshmi chapter and the closing blessing is cut out from Raja Ravi Varma, Goddess Lakshmi (1896), oil on canvas, Maharaja Fateh Singh Museum, Vadodara. Ravi Varma died in 1906; the painting is in the public domain.
